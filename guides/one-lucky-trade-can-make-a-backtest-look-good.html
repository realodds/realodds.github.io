One lucky trade can make a backtest look good

Published 29 October 2026 · Based on our own backtests

The best strategy we found, DOGE on 4-hour candles trading above and below its 100-period average, made R790 over two years on R300 trades. R530 of that came from a single trade. Without its 3 best trades, it made only R92.

What drove it

News, not the indicator. DOGE was about $0.165 on the night of the US election. It rallied on speculation about Elon Musk's government-efficiency role, and on 13 November 2024 Trump named Musk and Vivek Ramaswamy to lead a "Department of Government Efficiency", nicknamed DOGE, the same name as the coin (Kitco, 13 Nov 2024).

How to spot luck in any backtest

  • Remove the best 3 trades. If the profit disappears, it depended on luck.
  • Split the period in two. A real edge should make money in both halves.
  • Count how many strategies were tried. The best of 1,400 tests is mostly luck by definition.
