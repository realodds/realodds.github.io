Does counter-trading a losing strategy make money?

Published 29 October 2026 · Based on our own backtests

Usually not. When we reversed 32 strategies, 29 still lost money. The market part of a trade flips when you reverse it, but the fees don't: you pay them either way.

Why reversing doesn't create a winner

Every trade's result is the market move plus costs. Most strategies lost because the market part was close to zero and the costs were not. Reverse them, and you get roughly zero minus costs again.

How we tested: real Binance price history from 29 Oct 2024 to 29 Oct 2026, R300 per trade, 0.05% fee + 0.02% slippage per side on the full (leveraged) position, 0.01% funding per 8 hours held, and liquidation when a leveraged position is wiped out. Signals act on a closed candle and enter at the next candle's open. Past results do not predict future results.
