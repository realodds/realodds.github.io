Is a 90% win-rate trading bot real? We tested one

Published 29 October 2026 · Based on our own backtests

Yes, a 90%+ win rate is easy to get, and it can still lose money. Our "take R5 profit, then wait for the next reversal" bot hit its target 95–98% of the time without a stop-loss, and lost money in every leveraged test.

Bots sold with "90% win rate" claims usually work like this: take a small profit quickly and never close a losing trade. Most trades win a little. The few that don't either get liquidated or stay stuck for months.

What happened in our test

The bot followed the 4-hour trend, bought dips when RSI turned back up (sold rallies in a downtrend), and closed each trade at +R5 after costs on a R300 stake. With no stop-loss:

Why a high win rate isn't enough

One liquidation costs the whole R300 stake. That wipes out 60 winning trades of R5. At 1x there are almost no liquidations, but trades get stuck instead: one SOL trade stayed open for more than a year waiting for its R5.

Adding a stop-loss doesn't fix it. With a R5 stop, only about 44–45% of trades reached +R5 first at 1x, because fees and slippage tilt every trade slightly against you. Across 120 combinations of entry rule, leverage and stop, 119 lost money; the one exception spent its whole second year stuck in a single trade.

What to ask any bot seller

  • What is the average loss compared with the average win?
  • How many liquidations or stuck trades were there?
  • Are fees, slippage and funding included?
  • Can I see every trade, not just screenshots of winners?

How we tested: real Binance price history from 29 Oct 2024 to 29 Oct 2026, R300 per trade, 0.05% fee + 0.02% slippage per side on the full (leveraged) position, 0.01% funding per 8 hours held, and liquidation when a leveraged position is wiped out. Signals act on a closed candle and enter at the next candle's open. Past results do not predict future results.
