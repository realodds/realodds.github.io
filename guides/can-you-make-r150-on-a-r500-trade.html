Can you make R150 on a R500 crypto trade?

Published 29 October 2026 · Based on our own backtests

Sometimes, but not reliably. R150 on R500 is a 30% return, which needs 5–20x leverage within a day or a week. In two years of data, the chance of reaching +R150 before losing R150 was about a coin flip, slightly worse after fees.

Can a wider stop improve the odds?

A wider stop wins more often, but each loss gets bigger, so the average stays negative:

Does more starting capital help?

It helps you survive longer, not win more. We simulated one trade a week for a year, risking R150 per trade on SOL at 10x. Starting with R1,000, 46% of runs ran out of money. Starting with R5,000, almost none did, but only about 39% of runs finished the year ahead.

How we tested: real Binance price history from 29 Oct 2024 to 29 Oct 2026, R300 per trade, 0.05% fee + 0.02% slippage per side on the full (leveraged) position, 0.01% funding per 8 hours held, and liquidation when a leveraged position is wiped out. Signals act on a closed candle and enter at the next candle's open. Past results do not predict future results.
