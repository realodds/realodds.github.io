Strategy search is a Pro feature. It tests every strategy and direction on your coin, ranks them, and flags which held up in both halves of the data.

Uses your coin, candle size, history, leverage, stake and costs. Each strategy uses its default settings. When many strategies are tested, the best one is partly luck — check the "both halves" column.

Target odds is a Pro feature. Enter a profit goal and a risk amount and see how often the market actually reached it first.

Checks every possible entry (every candle, long and short) in the loaded data: did price reach your goal before your stop?
